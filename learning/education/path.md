# Education (contributing discipline)

A **contributing discipline** to the leadership-and-identity major. Provides the account of *how adults actually learn, grow, and transform* — directly bearing on what founder development can and cannot be.

See [`/curriculum.md`](../../curriculum.md), [`/through-line.md`](../../through-line.md).

## Lens on the through-line

If the through-line asks what a founder must *be*, then the question of *how a founder becomes* is an education question. Adult learning, transformative learning, expertise development, and the conditions under which mature change is possible — this discipline gives the major an empirical floor under any claim about founder development.

## How this discipline feeds the major

Leadership-development literature is full of claims about what founders should *be*. Much of it is silent on whether — and how — adults actually develop those capacities. Education research is where that answer lives. Without engaging it, the major's prescriptive claims about founder development are wishful.

## Literature map (initial sketch — to develop in `literature.md`)

- **Adult learning theory** (Knowles, andragogy) — how adults differ as learners
- **Transformative learning** (Mezirow, Cranton) — disorienting dilemmas, perspective transformation
- **Self-directed learning** (Tough, Brookfield) — what makes self-directed adult study actually work
- **Expertise development** (Ericsson, Chi) — deliberate practice, expert vs. novice
- **Reflective practice** (Schön) — knowing-in-action, reflection-in-action
- **Communities of practice** (Lave, Wenger) — situated learning, legitimate peripheral participation
- **Action learning / action research** (Revans, Reason) — learning by doing in real settings
