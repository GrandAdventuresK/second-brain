# Leadership & Identity

One of three active learning paths. See [`/curriculum.md`](../../curriculum.md) and [`/through-line.md`](../../through-line.md).

## Lens on the through-line

The **be** layer. The capacities the founder must develop because they're exactly what AI cannot supply — self-knowledge, courage, presence, judgment, the willingness to bear weight. As cognitive work compresses toward AI, leadership compresses toward the irreducibly human.

Working hypothesis: the founder of an AI-shaped firm is asked to be *more* human, not less. The work of this path is to find out what that means in practice.
