## Lens on the through-line

The **be** layer. The interior capacities the founder must develop — self-knowledge, courage, presence, judgment, the willingness to bear weight, the capacity for honest contact with self and others. As cognitive work compresses toward AI, leadership compresses toward the irreducibly human, and the founder's interior becomes the load-bearing variable.

## Working dissertation question

> **v0 (will narrow through Phase 1):**
>
> *What does it mean to lead a founder-led firm well during the AI-shaped transition of the 2020s — what interior capacities must the founder develop, what identity work must they do, and what organizational forms support that work?*

The question gets refined every quarter as the literature surfaces what's tractable and as Grand Adventures interventions surface what's real. By end of Phase 1 (~18–24 months in), the question will be defended in a written prospectus.

## Literature map (initial sketch — to develop in `literature.md`)

Schools / lineages on first pass:

- **Developmental psychology applied to leadership** (Kegan, Loevinger, Cook-Greuter, Torbert) — adult stages of meaning-making, action logics
- **Founder-identity work** (Colonna, Hsieh, Catmull, Lashinsky on founders) — coaching-derived accounts of founder interior
- **Adaptive leadership** (Heifetz, Linsky) — leadership as work, distinct from authority
- **Authentic leadership** (Bill George, Avolio) — interior alignment as leadership ground
- **Vulnerability and courage** (Brené Brown, David Whyte) — relational risk as leadership substrate
- **Contemplative / Eastern lineages** — presence, equanimity, non-attachment practices
- **Existential / phenomenological** (Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty applied to leadership) — what it is to be a founder, ontologically

**Live debates worth tracking:** developmental-stage models vs. trait/competency models; interior-first vs. behavior-first leadership development; the role of contemplative practice in leadership formation; whether "founder identity" is a meaningful category or a folk concept.
