# Systems Thinking (contributing discipline)

A **contributing discipline** to the leadership-and-identity major. Provides the analytical frame for seeing the firm-as-system the founder is inside of and acting on.

See [`/curriculum.md`](../../curriculum.md), [`/through-line.md`](../../through-line.md).

## Lens on the through-line

How the founder *sees and intervenes in the organization as a system* — feedback loops, stocks and flows, leverage points, emergent dynamics — rather than as an org chart. The frame that turns "the company" from a static structure into a dynamic system the founder leads.

## How this discipline feeds the major

A leadership argument that ignores firm-level dynamics collapses into individual psychology. Systems thinking gives the major its account of *what the founder is acting on* and *how interventions actually propagate*. Without it, the major's claims about "leading well" lack mechanism.

## Literature map (initial sketch — to develop in `literature.md`)

- **System Dynamics** (Forrester, Sterman) — quantitative, simulation, industrial dynamics
- **Learning Organization** (Senge, Argyris) — applied to org practice, double-loop learning
- **Meadows lineage** (Donella Meadows) — accessible synthesis, leverage points
- **Soft Systems Methodology** (Checkland) — qualitative, human activity systems
- **Cybernetics / Viable Systems** (Beer, Maturana) — control, viability, autopoiesis
- **Ackoff lineage** — interactive planning, idealized design
- **Complexity / CAS** (Santa Fe Institute, Mitchell, Holland, Page) — emergence, agent-based
- **Cynefin** (Snowden) — sensemaking across complexity domains
