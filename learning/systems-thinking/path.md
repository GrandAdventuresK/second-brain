# Systems Thinking

One of three active learning paths. See [`/curriculum.md`](../../curriculum.md) and [`/through-line.md`](../../through-line.md).

## Lens on the through-line

The **do** layer. Seeing the organization as a system — feedback loops, stocks and flows, leverage points, emergent dynamics — rather than as an org chart of boxes and arrows. Designing interventions that work *with* how the system actually behaves rather than how it looks.

Working hypothesis: most founder mistakes are systemic — the wrong loop reinforced, the wrong feedback delayed, the wrong stock optimized. Systems literacy is the difference between leading the organization and being its passenger.
