# areas/

Ongoing responsibilities you maintain — things with no end date but with a standard you hold.

Examples: your company, your health, your relationships, your home, your finances, a community you're part of.

## Areas vs. learning vs. topics

- **Area:** something you *tend* (no completion).
- **Learning topic:** something you're *actively studying* (has a path and a "done enough" feeling).
- **Topic page:** a *wiki view* of your thinking on something (no responsibility implied).

If you're not sure, ask: *am I responsible for this, or am I curious about this?*

## Shape

No fixed shape. Some areas are just an `index.md` with links to relevant notes and projects. Others grow sub-folders. Let the area's needs shape it.
