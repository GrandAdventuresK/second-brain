# Curriculum

The doctoral-shape program of study this vault is organized to support. Rigor-by-design without an institution: the architecture earns its bite from the work itself, from the [validation disciplines](validation.md), and from the public reception of the work.

This is one user's curriculum. If you're not me, treat it as an example of what self-directed doctoral-level study can look like.

## Through-line and dissertation question

The through-line is the lifelong question. See [`through-line.md`](through-line.md).

The dissertation question is a 3–5 year narrowing of it. The current draft lives inside the major path and gets refined every quarter as reading clarifies what's tractable and as Grand Adventures interventions surface what's real. Final form is defended in a written prospectus at end of Phase 1.

**Working dissertation question (v0):**

> *What does it mean to lead a founder-led firm well during the AI-shaped transition of the 2020s — what interior capacities must the founder develop, what identity work must they do, and what organizational forms support that work?*

The question lives in [`learning/leadership-and-identity/path.md`](learning/leadership-and-identity/path.md).

## Major + contributing disciplines

Doctoral programs have hierarchy. Equal-weight tracks is a master's shape. Doctoral shape is one major (where the original contribution lands) plus contributing disciplines that feed it.

| Role | Path | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Major** | [`leadership-and-identity/`](learning/leadership-and-identity/) | The dissertation contribution lives here. Founder identity, interior capacities, the *be* layer. |
| Contributing | [`ai-and-emerging-tech/`](learning/ai-and-emerging-tech/) | The substrate transition the leadership is happening *through*. Without this, the leadership argument is timeless and ungrounded. |
| Contributing | [`systems-thinking/`](learning/systems-thinking/) | The analytical frame for seeing the firm-as-system the founder is inside of and acting on. |
| Contributing | [`education/`](learning/education/) | How adults actually learn, grow, and transform — the empirical floor under any claim about what founder development *can* be. |
| Contributing | [`psychology/`](learning/psychology/) | Developmental, social, and organizational psychology — the empirical floor under any claim about human capacity and change. |

Why leadership-and-identity as major: the through-line centers on what a founder must *be*. The original contribution will be about founder development in the AI-shaped era. The other four fields are *contributing literatures* — the major can't be defended without engaging them, but the contribution lands in leadership.

The contributing disciplines have full `path.md` scaffolding but lighter expectations: smaller literature reviews (15–25 pages vs. the major's 40–60), fewer methods to train, no separate prospectus. They feed the major.

## Three phases

### Phase 1 — Coursework, literature, methods (18–24 months)

- **One literature review for the major** (40–60 pages), organized by school and debate. Establishes command of leadership/identity scholarship.
- **One lighter literature review per contributing discipline** (15–25 pages each). Maps the field well enough to engage it without becoming a specialist.
- **Methods training** — the major's methods deeply; contributing disciplines' methods to working competence.
- **Question narrowing** every quarter as reading clarifies what's tractable.
- **Quarterly comp papers** in service of the narrowing.

Phase 1 ends with the **prospectus** — a written defense of the dissertation question, its location in the leadership literature (with grounding in the contributing literatures), the proposed method, and the case for why it's worth 2–3 years of work.

### Phase 2 — Original work, case studies, framework development (2–3 years)

- **Predict-then-act interventions at Grand Adventures** (see [`validation.md`](validation.md)) — leadership and organizational interventions designed against your theory, with predictions logged before action.
- **Case studies of 5–10 peer founders / firms** — comparative material for the argument.
- **Framework iteration** — the draft contribution gets tested, broken, rebuilt with each experiment and case.
- **Continuing comp papers** building toward the dissertation argument.

Phase 2 ends when the framework holds across enough cases that you're ready to write the terminal product.

### Phase 3 — The dissertation (6–12 months)

Write the terminal product — book, framework, methodology — to publication standard. External validators (market, readership, adoption) deliver verdict over the following year.

Terminal output is **singular**, **dated**, **public**. Candidates:

- A book on founder development in the AI-shaped era.
- A documented framework for founder interior work that other founders adopt.
- A methodology taught somewhere beyond Grand Adventures.

## Methods training (named)

Doctoral programs train *how* to study, separately from *what* to study.

**Leadership & Identity (major) — deep training:**

- Case study method
- Phenomenological interview
- Developmental measurement (e.g. Kegan's subject-object interview)
- Longitudinal self-observation
- Coaching-as-inquiry

**Contributing disciplines — working competence:**

- *AI & Emerging Tech:* deep paper reading, benchmark interpretation, deployment case study
- *Systems Thinking:* causal loop diagramming, archetype analysis, leverage point analysis, intervention design with before/after
- *Education:* adult-learning theory application, transformative-learning analysis, reflective practice as method
- *Psychology:* developmental assessment, critical reading of empirical papers, distinguishing replicated from unreplicated claims

Each path's `path.md` tracks which methods have been *trained* (done on real questions), not just read about.

## Literatures, not reading lists

Each path builds a *reading map* organized by **schools and debates**, not by best books. The map names the lineages, the canonical works each points to, the live disagreements, and the gaps. Reading is clustered around sub-questions (5–15 sources in parallel), not chapter-by-chapter through one book.

Each path's reading map lives in `learning/<path>/literature.md` (created when you start building it).

## Five modes of work

The block ritual is the default (lecture-block). Other modes escalate when work calls for them: seminar, lab, problem-set, comp paper. See [`block-ritual.md`](block-ritual.md).

## Validation discipline (in place of a committee)

See [`validation.md`](validation.md). Two non-negotiable disciplines make markets and readers actually bite:

1. **Predict-before-acting at Grand Adventures** — every meaningful intervention preceded by a written prediction. Tests the *theory*, not just execution.
2. **Falsifiable claims + critic-seeking in public writing** — every comp paper makes claims that can be wrong, names what would falsify them, is actively shown to smart critics.

Without these the dissertation devolves into thoughtful self-flattery.

## Living faculty

Doctorate-level means being in conversation with people currently doing the work. Each path tracks 3–5 living thinkers whose current work is followed, cited, and written *in response to*. See each `path.md`.

## Designing for seriousness, not duration

You don't know if this will run for 10 days, 10 months, or 10 years. The architecture is designed for the *intended seriousness*, not the worst-case persistence.

- If you stop in 10 days the spine docs still read as a manifesto.
- If you stop in 10 months Phase 1 is partially built and the literature reviews are working artifacts.
- If you go the distance, the architecture supports the full dissertation arc.

Build for the version that requires the most rigor; the shorter cases inherit it automatically.
