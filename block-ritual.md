# The Block Ritual

The single repeating practice that turns this vault from a vault into a forge.

## Why a ritual

You learn in blocks, not daily streaks. Your time-of-day shifts. You take in a lot — books, articles, podcasts, video — and most of it never gets pressed into your own words. A ritual works when its shape is identical every time, so showing up doesn't require deciding what to do.

## The rule that makes it work

**Input without forging doesn't count.**

A block is only "done" when something has been pressed from a source into your own words in `notes/` and linked to at least one other note or topic. Reading, listening, watching — none of it counts on its own. The forge step is non-negotiable. This is what closes the follow-through loop and builds the articulation muscle at the same time.

## Shape of a block

Roughly 60–90 minutes. Same four steps, every time.

```
1. Triage inbox/           (~5 min)
2. Press 1–3 sources       (~20 min each)
3. Advance one path        (~20 min)
4. Close                   (~2 min)
```

### 1. Triage `inbox/`

Open `inbox/`. For each item:

- **Keep & route** — move it to `sources/<kind>/` (article, book, podcast, video, talk) with whatever capture you already have.
- **Already a thought of yours?** Drop it directly into `notes/`.
- **Doesn't earn its keep?** Delete it. No archive.

Goal: leave `inbox/` empty or near-empty. Don't over-tag, don't over-name — `kebab-case.md` and move on.

### 2. Press 1–3 sources

This is the forge. Pick 1–3 items from `sources/` — usually the ones still warm. For each:

- Read or skim until you have *one thing worth saying back*.
- Write **one atomic note** in `notes/` — in your own words, not the author's. One idea per file.
- Link it: at minimum to the source (`[[source-name]]`), and to one existing note or topic. If nothing exists to link to, create the topic stub.

If you can't say it back in your own words, you didn't get it. Read less, press more.

### 3. Advance one active path

You have **three active learning paths** at a time. Right now:

- `learning/leadership-and-identity/`
- `learning/ai-and-emerging-tech/`
- `learning/systems-thinking/`

Pick one — usually whichever is most alive for you today. Add to its `synthesis/`. The synthesis is a running compression of what you now know in that domain that you didn't before. It can be:

- a paragraph that updates your current understanding
- a diagram or map
- a comparison between two ideas
- a question you're now able to ask sharply

Anything that compresses. The synthesis is what you'd hand someone if they asked, "what do you actually think about this?"

### 4. Close

In the path's `path.md`, jot one line about where to resume — the question you're chasing, the source queued next, the synthesis section that's still soft. This is what makes the *next* block start fast.

## Bounds

- **Max 3 active learning paths.** When one matures (synthesis is teachable, you can hand it to a peer), a slot opens. Domains not on a path stay ambient — they flow through `inbox/` and `sources/` and may earn a `notes/` atom, but get no structured path until they take a slot.
- **Founder obligations (finance, tech, marketing)** live in `areas/`, not `learning/`. One page per area, kept minimally healthy — current state, the one thing to know better right now, 2–3 trusted sources. They earn a learning path only if one becomes the bottleneck.
- **No daily streaks.** Block-based. A missed day is not a broken streak; there is no streak.

## Monthly: `tend-topics`

Once a month — or whenever `inbox/` and `notes/` feel heavy — run the `tend-topics` ritual. It surfaces orphan notes, stale topic pages, and emerging clusters. Approve the proposals; let the topic layer accrete. Patterns you didn't plan for will start to show. That's the system mirroring you back to yourself.

## This is the *default* block

A block in this shape is a **lecture-block** — input pressed into your own words. There are four other modes (seminar, lab, problem-set, comp paper) that escalate when the work calls for them. See [`curriculum.md`](curriculum.md) for the full set.

## What this ritual is fixing

- **Scattered capture** → one `inbox/`, drained every block.
- **Articulation gap** → the forge step requires your own words, every block.
- **Follow-through gap** → "done" has a clear definition that doesn't depend on motivation.
- **Diffuse attention** → 3-path cap forces choice; everything else stays ambient without guilt.
